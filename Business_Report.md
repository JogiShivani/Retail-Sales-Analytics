# Retail Sales Analytics (Python + SQL + Excel + Power BI)

## Business Problem

ShivMart Retail Pvt. Ltd. wants to analyze its sales performance across India.

**Objectives**

* Identify top-selling products.
* Identify high-profit categories.
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
* Compare sales across regions and states.
* Measure the impact of discounts on profit.
* Build an interactive dashboard for management.

## Project Objective

Build an end-to-end retail analytics solution using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI by generating datasets, performing data analysis, and developing an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Generation (Python)

* Generated retail datasets for customers, products, and orders.
* Used Python and Pandas to create realistic sales data.
* Exported datasets as CSV files.

### 2. Data Analysis (SQL)

* Imported datasets into MySQL.
* Performed sales aggregation and business analysis.
* Identified top-selling products and profit trends.
* Compared sales across categories, states, and regions.

### 3. Data Preparation & Analysis (Excel / Google Sheets)

* Imported retail sales data from CSV files.
* Created pivot tables for category, state, and product analysis.
* Built charts for business insights.
* Prepared data for SQL and Power BI analysis.

### 4. Data Visualization (Power BI)

Built an interactive dashboard containing:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* Sales Trend (2025)
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Sales Distribution by Category
* Top States by Sales
* Interactive filters for Category, State, and Order Date
